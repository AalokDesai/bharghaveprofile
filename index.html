About

Training built around the person, not a template.

I'm a Certified Advanced Personal Trainer and CPR & AED-certified fitness professional working in Mumbai. My focus is helping people improve their strength, body composition, movement quality, and confidence through training that's structured and progressive rather than generic.

Before writing a single program, I take time to understand a client's goals, current fitness level, movement ability, and training history. Good coaching starts with listening.
